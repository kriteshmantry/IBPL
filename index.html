Built for the courts of India

The IBPL started in 2024 with a simple idea: the talent playing on school courts, college gyms and neighbourhood cages across the country deserves a professional stage.

Every franchise runs an open trial in its home region, every team keeps at least six homegrown players on its roster, and every game is streamed free. Our players are paid, insured and coached by staff certified to FIBA standards.
